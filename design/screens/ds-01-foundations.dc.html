Abyss & Lantern

The design system for Meduza — restaurant, lounge and bar in Nha Trang. Two lights in one room: the cool glow of the jellyfish tank and the warm glow of the rattan lantern.

Version 1.0 November 2026 EN · RU · VI
01 · Principles

Quiet interface. Glowing content.

i.

Darkness is the canvas

Like the room after sunset, the UI recedes into deep navy. Photography, dishes and the booking action are the only things that glow.

ii.

Two lights, two moods

Glow (cyan) belongs to the lounge, bar and hookah. Lantern (amber) belongs to dining and brunch. Never more than one accent leads a screen.

iii.

Calm, precise, tactile

Apple-grade clarity: one primary action per screen, generous space, real materials — frosted glass, soft depth, motion that settles like water.

02 · Color

Sampled from the room

Abyss neutrals come from the tank wall and bar ceiling; Pearl neutrals from the cream plaster and rattan; accents from the jellyfish light and the paper lanterns. Ratios are WCAG contrast against the ground shown.

06 · Photography Shoot the light, not the room.
Night only, no flash. Keep blacks deep; let the tank or the lanterns be the light source. Crop tight on texture: rattan, fringe curtains, wave panels.
